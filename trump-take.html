How this counter works

  • The ticking number is an annual income flow — money coming in — not net worth. It counts up from Trump's Jan 20, 2025 inauguration at $3.4B/year ($107.74/second).
  • $3.4B/yr = Trump's documented personal income ($2.2B, Fortune) + a conservative estimate of additional Trump-family gains not on his personal filing (~$1.2B, from the House Judiciary report's $11.6B holdings and $800M+ H1-2025 crypto sales).
  • Cabinet figures are net worth (a stock), not a yearly flow, so they are shown as context and are deliberately not added to the ticking counter — that would mix a one-time balance with an annual rate.
  • Figures are drawn from public reporting and financial disclosures. They are estimates; where sources disagree we use the more conservative number. All dollar amounts are USD.
